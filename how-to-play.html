How To Play

A simple guide to understand YS PLAY, numbers, charts and virtual game points.

Getting Started

New to YS PLAY? No problem. Follow the steps below to understand the basic process, check the latest chart and keep track of your virtual game activity.

How It Works

01

Contact Us

Message us through WhatsApp or Telegram to get started and receive the required information.

02

Get Your Player ID

After contacting us, you may receive a unique Player ID. Keep your ID safe for future reference.

03

Receive Virtual Points

Virtual game points can be used within the website's game experience according to the applicable game rules.

04

Choose Your Number

Select an available two-digit number and use your virtual points according to the rules of the game.

05

Check Today's Chart

Once the round is updated, visit the Home page and check the Today's Chart section for the latest displayed result.

06

Track Your Game Points

Keep track of your virtual points and activity according to the game's rules and information displayed on the website.

07

Wait For The Next Round

The website timer shows the next update cycle. When the timer reaches zero, the next round can begin automatically.

Need Help?

If you have questions about your Player ID, game points or how the website works, contact us through the available channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Player ID?

A Player ID is a unique reference number used to identify your game profile.

Where can I see the latest result?

Open the Home page and check the Today's Chart section.

How do I check previous results?

Open the Old Charts tab to view previously displayed chart information.

What happens when the timer ends?

The timer resets automatically and the next update cycle begins.

Are the points real money?

No. The points displayed on this website are virtual game points and have no cash value.
